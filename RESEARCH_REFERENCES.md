# Verified sources vs newly proposed mechanisms

These bibliographic identities and publisher pages were checked on 2026-11-12. A publisher link verifies publication metadata, **not** the correctness of the new fusion mechanism. No unsupported exact equation number is invented.

1. **Audun Jøsang (2016). _Subjective Logic: A Formalism for Reasoning Under Uncertainty_.** Springer. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-319-42337-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-42337-1). Relevant sections: **Elements of Subjective Opinions** (pp. 19–50) and **Belief Fusion** (pp. 207–236). Supports use of binomial subjective-opinion terminology. Our pooling/conflict equations are NOT copied from or claimed to be a Jøsang operator.
2. **Glenn W. Brier (1950). _Verification of Forecasts Expressed in Terms of Probability_.** _Monthly Weather Review_, 78(1), pp. 1–3. DOI: [10.1175/1520-0493(1950)078<0001:VOFEIT>2.0.CO;2](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/mwre/78/1/1520-0493_1950_078_0001_vofeit_2_0_co_2.xml). Supports probability forecast assessment by Brier score; our service does not calculate a trustworthy IF Brier skill.
3. **Fei Tony Liu, Kai Ming Ting, Zhi-Hua Zhou (2008). _Isolation Forest_.** Eighth IEEE International Conference on Data Mining, pp. 413–422. DOI: [10.1109/ICDM.2008.17](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDM.2008.17). Supports anomaly detection by isolation, not interpreting an anomaly score as fraud probability.
4. **Scott M. Lundberg, Su-In Lee (2017). _A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions_.** Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30. [Publisher paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/8a20a8621978632d76c43dfd28b67767-Abstract.html). Establishes SHAP. The implemented additive tree-path contributions are explicitly NOT SHAP.
5. **Yonatan Geifman, Ran El-Yaniv (2017). _Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks_.** Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30. [Publisher page](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html). Documents reject-option selective prediction; our decision cutoffs and abstention conditions are not directly from the paper.

**Novelty status:** The context-aware attribution discount, shared-feature dependency heuristic, IF-applicability rule, pooled support, conflict proxy, and uncertainty expression are PROPOSED design elements, unvalidated and not claimed as published formulas. A systematic prior-art review is still necessary for any novelty claim.
